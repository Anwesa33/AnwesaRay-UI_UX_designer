Case study

MedHelper

Families lose track of medicines and medical history at home. MedHelper keeps both in one place, without assuming the patient can manage it alone.

Mobile appUX research
Role
UX designer
Type
Mobile app
Focus
Research, onboarding
Tools
Figma

The problem

Most medicine apps assume patients manage their own history. At home, it is usually a family member who keeps track for someone else.

What I did

I researched adherence and medicine waste, then designed onboarding wireframes built around “who do you look after?”
